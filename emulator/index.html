Rangoli Rush

Pinch coloured powder, brush glowing petals and light up a rangoli — seated, hands only, right on your table.

Hands only · seated · ~5 minute sessions
  • Pinch inside a bowl to scoop powder (or look at a bowl and pinch anywhere).
  • Hold the pinch and brush over a petal of the matching colour.
  • Flick — release fast toward the board to throw powder.
